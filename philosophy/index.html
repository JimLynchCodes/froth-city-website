Philosophy

Four questions. No exceptions.

Every company we cover is put through the same framework, whether it's a market darling or a forgotten industrial.

01

Profit margins matter the most

Current and future. Revenue growth without a credible path to margin is just a more expensive way to lose money. We model unit economics first and narratives never.

02

Durability

Can the business still earn its margins in five years? We look for moats, switching costs, and pricing power — and short the companies whose only moat is a press cycle.

03

Macro headwinds

Rates, consumer stress, input costs, regulation. A business that only works in a zero-rate, everything-up world is not a business we trust.

04

Punish excessive debt

Leverage turns a bad quarter into a fatal one. Refinancing walls, floating-rate exposure, and convertible overhangs weigh heavily in every rating we issue.
