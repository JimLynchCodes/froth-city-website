Our Firm

Independent research from the tip of Manhattan.

Froth City Research is an independent sell-side equity research firm headquartered in Battery Park, a few blocks from where the market was born.

Founder

Jim Lynch

Founder & Head of Research

Froth City was founded by Jim Lynch, a legendary software engineer and mathematician who spent his career building systems where the numbers have to add up.

He brings the same rigor to public markets: strip away the narrative, model the cash flows, and ask one question — does this business ever make money?

We take no investment banking fees and accept no payment from the companies we cover. Our only clients are our readers.
