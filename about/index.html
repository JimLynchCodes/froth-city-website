Founder

Jim Lynch

Founder & Head of Research

Froth City was founded by Jim Lynch, a legendary software engineer and mathematician who spent his career building systems where the numbers have to add up.

He thinks chartists are foolish gamblers. The real long-term money, in his view, is made by working out which companies will have the best cash flows over time, and which will have the worst.

When he is not doing research, he enjoys playing tennis, working out, and eating hemp seeds.

We take no investment banking fees and accept no payment from the companies we cover. Our only clients are our readers.
