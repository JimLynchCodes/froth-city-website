The research wire.

A running log of every publication. Follow along as new reports drop.

  1. Oct 19, 2026 ▼ Short
    CoreWeave: Debt, Rates, and the Data-Center Crossfire

    CoreWeave's scheduled debt principal was $35.6 billion at June 30, 2026, and quarterly interest expense already exceeds operating income. The book is concentrated in AI infrastructure demand, including a large commitment from OpenAI, while local fights over power, water, and zoning are slowing the data-center buildout the debt is funding.

  2. Oct 12, 2026 ▼ Short
    VaporStack AI: A Wrapper Around a Wrapper

    An 'AI-native' platform with 11% gross margins, rising compute costs, and customer concentration that would make a lender blush. We see no credible path to operating profit before the convertible notes come due in 2028.

  3. Oct 5, 2026 ▼ Short
    GreenVolt Mobility: Burning Cash at Highway Speed

    Deliveries are up, but every vehicle shipped loses roughly $31k. With $2.4B of debt and higher-for-longer rates, dilution is the business model.

  4. Sep 28, 2026 ▲ Long
    Harborline Logistics: Boring, Durable, Underpriced

    A 40-year-old freight operator with 18% operating margins, net cash, and pricing power the market ignores while chasing hype. Durability at a discount.

  5. Sep 8, 2026 ▼ Short
    QuantumLeap Holdings: Qubits Without Customers

    A $9B market cap on $14M of revenue, most of it government grants. Management's roadmap pushes profitability past the horizon of any reasonable model.

  6. Aug 17, 2026 ▲ Long
    Cedar Mill Industrials: Margin Expansion Hiding in Plain Sight

    Mix shift toward aftermarket parts is lifting gross margin 300bps per year. Low leverage and modest macro sensitivity round out the case.

  7. Jul 21, 2026 ▼ Short
    SnackPal: Unit Economics That Don't Deliver

    Contribution margin is negative in 9 of 12 markets once promotions are included. A refinancing wall in 2027 meets a consumer under pressure.

  8. Jun 30, 2026 ▼ Short
    Metaverse Realty Trust: Digital Land, Real Debt

    Virtual land valuations marked to wishful thinking, financed with very real floating-rate debt. We estimate NAV at a fraction of the current price.
