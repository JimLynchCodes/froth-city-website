    Oracle: The Software Franchise Is Funding the Build

    Uranium: The Fuel Works. The Equity Has to Wait

    The physics is the bull case. The short is the equity: new mines take 10 to 20 years, 2025 term contracting was still below replacement, and the convertibles we could verify are covered by cash shareholders already provided.

    CoreWeave: A Ranked Short

    The short, in order: a principal schedule the operations do not fund, an interest bill larger than operating income, concentrated customers, a six-year GPU life, and power that is not yet delivered. Community opposition and insider selling are not at the top of the list.

    WW International: A Smaller Loan on a Shrinking Business

    Uniti: The REIT Ended. The Debt Did Not.
