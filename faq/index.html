Frequently Asked Questions

Can I read your reports for free? +

Reports become free one month after release. Until then they may only be accessed by subscribed members. The Weekly 5/5 and founder meetings are paid.

When does the Weekly 5/5 go out? +

Every Monday morning before the market opens: our top 5 equities to short and top 5 to go long.

What does the $849 plan include? +

The Weekly 5/5, plus a monthly one-hour meeting with the founders and lead analysts for personalized research. Reports become free one month after release. Until then they may only be accessed by subscribed members.

Do you hold positions in the companies you cover? +

We may. Every report discloses our position at the time of publication.

Are you paid by the companies you cover? +

Never. We take no banking fees or corporate payments. Our only clients are our readers.

Is this investment advice? +

No. Our research is opinion based on public information. Do your own diligence.
